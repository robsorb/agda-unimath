## Idea

Given types `A` and `B` and subtypes `S ⊆ A` and `T ⊆ B`, a pair
`(a , b) : A × B` is in the
{{#concept "Cartesian product" disambiguation="of subtypes" WDID=Q173740 WD="Cartesian product" Agda=product-subtype}}
of `S` and `T` if `a ∈ S` and `b ∈ T`.
