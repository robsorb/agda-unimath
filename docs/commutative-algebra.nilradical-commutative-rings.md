## Idea

The **nilradical** of a
[commutative ring](commutative-algebra.commutative-rings.md) is the
[ideal](commutative-algebra.ideals-commutative-rings.md) consisting of all
[nilpotent elements](ring-theory.nilpotent-elements-rings.md).
