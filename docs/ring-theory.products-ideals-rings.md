## Idea

The **product** of two [ideals](ring-theory.ideals-rings.md) `I` and `J` in a
[ring](ring-theory.rings.md) is the ideal
[generated by](ring-theory.ideals-generated-by-subsets-rings.md) all products of
elements in `I` and `J`.
