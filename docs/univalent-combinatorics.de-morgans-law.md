## Idea

Given a [finite](univalent-combinatorics.finite-types.md) family of
[De Morgan types](logic.de-morgan-types.md) `P : Fin k → De-Morgan-Type`, then
the _finitary De Morgan's law_

```text
  ¬ (∀ i, P i) ⇒ ∃ i, ¬ (P i)
```

holds.
