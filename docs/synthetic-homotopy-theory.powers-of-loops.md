## Idea

The **`n`-th power of a [loop](synthetic-homotopy-theory.loop-spaces.md)** `ω`
in a type `A` is defined by [iterated](foundation.iterating-functions.md)
[concatenation](foundation.identity-types.md) of `ω` with itself.
