## Idea

The {{#concept "exponential" Agda=exp-ℕ WD="exponentiation" WDID=Q33456}} $m^n$
of two [natural numbers](elementary-number-theory.natural-numbers.md) is the
number obtained by multiplying $m$ with itself $n$ times.
