## Idea

The **kernel** of a [ring homomorphism](ring-theory.homomorphisms-rings.md)
`f : R → S` is the [ideal](ring-theory.ideals-rings.md) of `R` consisting of all
elements `x : R` equipped with an identification `f x ＝ 0`.
