## Idea

Two [tuples](lists.tuples.md) can be
{{#concept "concatenated" Disambiguation="pairs of tuples" Agda=concat-tuple}}
to form a single tuple. Given a tuple `(u_1, ..., u_n)` and a tuple
`(v_1, ..., v_m)`, their concatenation is the tuple
`(u_1, ..., u_n, v_1, ..., v_m)`.
