## Idea

A **spectrum** is a [sequence](lists.sequences.md) of
[pointed types](structured-types.pointed-types.md) `A` equipped with an
equivalence

```text
  Aₙ ≃∗ ΩAₙ₊₁
```

for each `n : ℕ`.
