## Idea

A [globular type](globular-types.globular-types.md) is
{{#concept "reflexive" Disambiguation="globular type" Agda=is-reflexive-globular-structure}}
if every $n$-cell `x` comes with a choice of $(n+1)$-cell from `x` to `x`.
