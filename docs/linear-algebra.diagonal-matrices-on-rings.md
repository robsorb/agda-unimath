## Definitions

A
{{#concept "diagonal matrix" Agda=diagonal-matrix-Ring WD="diagonal matrix" WDID=Q332791}}
is a [matrix](linear-algebra.matrices.md) whose only nonzero elements are on the
diagonal of the matrix.
