## Idea

An homomorphism of
[large meet-semilattices](order-theory.large-meet-semilattices.md) from `K` to
`L` is an
[order preserving map](order-theory.order-preserving-maps-large-posets.md) from
`K` to `L` which preserves meets and the top element.
