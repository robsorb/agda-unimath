## Idea

Consider a subuniverse `P` and a type `A` in `P`. A **Π-decomposition** of `A`
into types in `P` consists of a type `X` in `P` and a family `Y` of types in `P`
indexed over `X`, equipped with an equivalence

```text
  A ≃ Π X Y.
```
