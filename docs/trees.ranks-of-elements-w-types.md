## Idea

Consider two elements `x` and `y` of a W-type `𝕎 A B`. We say that the **rank**
of `x` is at most the rank of `y` if for every element `x' ∈ x` and for every
element `y' ∈ y` the rank of `x'` is at most the rank of `y'`.
