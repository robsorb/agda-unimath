## Idea

Given two [truncation levels](foundation-core.truncation-levels.md) $k$ and $r$,
the
{{#concept "maximum" Disambiguation="binary maximum of truncation levels" Agda=max-𝕋}}
of $k$ and $r$ is the largest of the two.
