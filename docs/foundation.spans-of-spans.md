## Idea

Given two [spans](foundation.spans.md) `F` and `G` from `A` to `B`

```text
  S ------> B
  | F       ∧
  |         |
  ∨       G |
  A <------ T,
```

then a {{#concept "span of spans" Disambiguation="of types" Agda=span-of-spans}}
from `F` to `G` is a span `S <--- W ---> T` such that the diagram

```text
  S ------> B
  |  ↖      ∧
  |    W    |
  ∨      ↘  |
  A <------ T
```

commutes.
