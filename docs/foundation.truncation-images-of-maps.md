## Idea

The **`k`-truncation image** of a map `f : A → B` is the type `trunc-im k f`
that fits in the (`k`-connected,`k`-truncated) factorization of `f`. It is
defined as the type

```text
  trunc-im k f := Σ (y : B), type-trunc k (fiber f y)
```
