## Idea

The **fiber** of a directed tree `T` at a node `x` consists of all nodes `y`
equipped with a walk `w : walk T y x`. An edge from `(y, w)` to `(z , v)`
consists of an edge `e : edge T x y` such that `w ＝ cons-walk e v`.
