## Idea

An **algebra** over a [ring](ring-theory.rings.md) `R` consists of an
[`R`-module](linear-algebra.left-modules-rings.md) `M` equipped with a binary
operation `x y ↦ xy : M → M → M` such that

```text
  (xy)z  = x(yz)
  r(xy)  = (rx)y
  r(xy)  = x(ry)
  (x+y)z = xz+yz
  x(y+z) = xy+xz
```
