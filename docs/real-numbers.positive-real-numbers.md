## Idea

A [real number](real-numbers.dedekind-real-numbers.md) is
{{#concept "positive" Disambiguation="real number" Agda=is-positive-ℝ}} if zero
is [strictly less than](real-numbers.strict-inequality-real-numbers.md) it.
