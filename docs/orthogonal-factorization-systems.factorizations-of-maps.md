## Idea

A **factorization** of a map `f : A → B` is a pair of maps `g : X → B` and
`h : A → X` such that their composite `g ∘ h` is `f`.

```text
       X
      ∧ \
   h /   \ g
    /     ∨
  A -----> B
      f
```

We use diagrammatic order and say the map `h` is the _left_ and `g` the _right_
map of the factorization.
