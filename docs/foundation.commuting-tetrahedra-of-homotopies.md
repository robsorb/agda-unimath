## Idea

A
{{#concept "commuting tetrahedron of homotopies" Agda=coherence-tetrahedron-homotopies}}
is a commuting diagram of the form

```text
             top
       f ----------> g
       |  \       ∧  |
       |    \   /    |
  left |      /      | right
       |    /   \    |
       ∨  /       ∨  ∨
       h ----------> i.
            bottom
```

where `f`, `g`, `h`, and `i` are functions.
