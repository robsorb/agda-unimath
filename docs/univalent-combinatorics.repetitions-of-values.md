## Idea

A **repetition of values** of a map `f : A → B` consists of a
[pair of distinct elements](foundation.pairs-of-distinct-elements.md) `x ≠ y` of
`A` that get mapped to the [same](foundation-core.identity-types.md) element in
`B`: `f x ＝ f y`.
