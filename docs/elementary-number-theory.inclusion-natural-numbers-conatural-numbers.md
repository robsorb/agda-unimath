## Idea

The
{{#concept "inclusion of the nautural numbers into the conatural numbers" Agda=conatural-ℕ}}
is the inductively defined map

```text
  conatural-ℕ : ℕ → ℕ∞
```

that sends zero to zero, and the successor of a natural number to the successor
of its inclusion.
