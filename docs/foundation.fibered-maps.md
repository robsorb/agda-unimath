## Idea

Consider a diagram of the form

```text
    A         B
    |         |
  f |         | g
    ∨         ∨
    X ------> Y
         i
```

A **fibered map** from `f` to `g` over `i` is a map `h : A → B` such that the
square `i ∘ f ~ g ∘ h` [commutes](foundation-core.commuting-squares-of-maps.md).
