## Idea

The **product** of two
[subsets](commutative-algebra.subsets-commutative-rings.md) `S` and `T` of a
[commutative ring](commutative-algebra.commutative-rings.md) `A` is the subset
consistng of products `xy` where `x ∈ S` and `y ∈ T`.
