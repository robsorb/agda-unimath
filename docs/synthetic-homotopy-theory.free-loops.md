## Idea

A **free loop** in a type `X` consists of a point `x : X` and an
[identification](foundation.identity-types.md) `x ＝ x`. The type of free loops
in `X` is [equivalent](foundation-core.equivalences.md) to the type of maps
`𝕊¹ → X`.
