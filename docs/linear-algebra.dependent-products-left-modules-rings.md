## Idea

Given a [ring](ring-theory.rings.md) `R` and a family of
[left modules](linear-algebra.left-modules-rings.md) `Mᵢ` over `R` indexed by
`i : I`, the dependent product `Π (i : I) Mᵢ` is a left module over `R`.
