## Idea

A family of types `B : A → UU l` is said to be
{{#concept "local" Disambiguation="family of types at a map" Agda=is-local-family}}
at a map `f : X → Y`, or **`f`-local**, if every
[fiber](foundation-core.fibers-of-maps.md) is.
