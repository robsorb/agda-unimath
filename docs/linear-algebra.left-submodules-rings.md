## Idea

A
{{#concept "left submodule" Disambiguation="of a left module over a ring" Agda=is-left-submodule-prop-Ring Agda=left-submodule-Ring}}
of a [left module](linear-algebra.left-modules-rings.md) `M` over a
[ring](ring-theory.rings.md) `R` is a
[subset](linear-algebra.subsets-left-modules-rings.md) of `M` that is closed
under addition and multiplication by a scalar from `R`.
