## Idea

A
{{#concept "commutative semigroup" WDID=Q27672715 WD="commutative semigroup" Agda=Commutative-Semigroup}}
is a [semigroup](group-theory.semigroups.md) `G` in which `xy = yx` for all
`x y : G`.
