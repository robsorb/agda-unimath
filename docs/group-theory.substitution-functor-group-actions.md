## Idea

Given a [group homomorphism](group-theory.homomorphisms-groups.md) `f : G → H`
and an [`H`-set](group-theory.group-actions.md) `Y`, we obtain a `G`-action on
`Y` by `g,x ↦ f(g)x`. This operation is functorial in `Y`.
