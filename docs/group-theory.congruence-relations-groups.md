## Idea

A congruence relation on a group `G` is an equivalence relation `≡` on `G` such
that for every `x1 x2 y1 y2 : G` such that `x1 ≡ x2` and `y1 ≡ y2` we have
`x1 · y1 ≡ x2 · y2`.
