## Idea

A **generating set** of a [group](group-theory.groups.md) `G` is a subset
`S ⊆ G` such that the
[subgroup generated by `S`](group-theory.subgroups-generated-by-subsets-groups.md)
is the [full subgroup](group-theory.full-subgroups.md).
